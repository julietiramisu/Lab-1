My love letter to my city

A Day in Toronto

From the first cup of matcha for the day to the dim street lights at late night, I find peace in the ordinary moments throughout the day - a fun and youthful city, full of movement, and the little things that make me feel alive.

Slow Morning, Fast City

My day begins right when the sun just wakes up. The crispy air, cars honking on the street, and the city starts getting in movement. I run outside with my avocado toast in hand, sprinting straight to class; I might be late for my 9AM one!

2:00 PM - Matcha Time

The streets are filled with people. Office workers biking down Spadina Avenue, conversations are all over the place, and youngsters laughing, holding a milk tea in their hands. I also just finsihed my class, stopping by a café, ordering my usual matcha latte.

Toronto Slow Afternoon

After school, I like to wander for a bit around town. The fresh cool air in the ancient Chinatown feels familiar, the roads bathed in afternoon light, storefronts glow in the sun, and every corner just seems very nice and joyful.

7:00 PM - Dinner Time

As the sun falls lower, the city transforms. Buildings begin to glow, windows fill with warm golden light, and the streets become louder with mumbles, clinking glasses, and fun excitement for dinner plans. I meet up with my friends in Ossington, and the night starts to feel like a Saturday night.

My go-to dish in Ossington

We talk over pasta, pizza, and something sweet to finish the meal. There is always room for one more joke, one more story, and one more late-night conversation. The city feels bigger when you are sharing it with the people you love.

10:00 PM - Bed Time

Eventually, me and my friends part ways. The sky is dark now, most windows have turned pitch black, and the world starts falling asleep. I come home, get unready, and slip into my warm cozy bed.

Goodnight, Toronto

The night ends peacefully, but not fully enough to not keep the lingering feeling around in my heart. The city goes still outside my apartment, but the thoughts in my head are still buzzing slightly.

7:00 AM - The Next Morning

Morning arrives once again, same as yesterday. The clear sky brightens up, the air feels crispy again, and the city is so ready to begin all over. I step outside once again, bathing in the morning sunlight.

And the story continues

Would you like to join me again for another hangout downtown? The city is constantly moving and changing, but my love for it will always stay the same ♡.
